Forge a website in five steps

SiteForge turns a short conversation into a finished, professional site. Here is the whole journey.

⏱ First question → live site in under 30 minutes
  1. 01

    Start a project

    Add the client's name. SiteForge creates the project and hands you a private interview link to share.

  2. 02

    The guided interview

    The client answers a few smart questions about their business, goals, brand, and tone. It adapts as they go — no blank forms.

    saves automatically · resume anytime
  3. 03

    Review their content

    SiteForge pulls in the client's existing website, photos, hours, and details. You confirm exactly what to keep — nothing is used until you approve it.

  4. 04

    Generate the site

    A fitting theme, real written copy, and a bespoke color-and-type identity come together into an instant live preview.

    Lighthouse 90+ · mobile-ready · accessible
  5. 05

    Refine, then publish

    Change anything in plain English — “make it warmer,” “use the bold theme.” When it's right, publish in one click. Every version is saved, so you can roll back anytime.
